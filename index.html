How It Operates

The 3-Tier Multi-Agent Reasoning Pipeline

Rather than treating an LLM as a single-turn oracle, CogniFlow separates concerns into specialized autonomous nodes that deliberate, challenge, and synthesize truth from unstructured enterprise corpuses.

01

Long-Context Ingestion & Hierarchical Mapping

High-fidelity OCR and semantic layout analysis preserve tables, footnotes, and inter-clause references within the model's native 200k token window.

02

Chain-of-Logic Deliberation & Peer Review

Analytical sub-agents independently identify obligations, compliance breaches, or financial anomalies, then challenge each other's conclusions.

03

Deterministic Output & System Integration

Verified findings are formatted into structured JSON schemas, triggering automated Jira tickets, email alerts, or ERP ledger adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does CogniFlow differ from standard Vector RAG? ▼

Standard RAG breaks documents into isolated 500-token chunks, completely losing cross-page context (e.g. when Clause 14 references an exception defined on page 180). CogniFlow loads the entire comprehensive context into next-generation frontier reasoning windows (200,000+ tokens) and deploys multi-agent critics for exact holistic validation.

Which foundation models power the reasoning engine? ▼

We leverage Anthropic's state-of-the-art Claude 3.5 Sonnet and Opus models as our primary reasoning engines due to their unmatched mathematical, code, and long-context comprehension benchmarks.

Is customer data used to train AI models? ▼

Never. We enforce Zero Data Retention (ZDR) across all model endpoints. Your contracts, financial filings, and proprietary datasets remain strictly confidential and encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.
